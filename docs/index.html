How the prediction works

Your plan is a grid of about 1 m tiles. For every tile, WifiLens traces a straight line from the router and adds up what's in the way.

RSSI = A − 10·n·log₁₀(d) − Σ walls
  1. Distance fades the signal; n sets how fast (about 3 in a typical home).
  2. Walls on the way each subtract their material's loss, from glass to concrete.
  3. Your measurements from a walk survey tune A and n to your home.
  4. Best spot tries every tile and keeps the one that's best for your weakest device.

Private by design

WifiLens doesn't need to know who you are. Your floor plans, scans and history never leave your phone.

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Location used only to list Wi-Fi, never stored
