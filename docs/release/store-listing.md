# Play store listing

**App name** (30 max): WifiLens: Wi-Fi Analyzer

**Short description** (80 max):
Map your home's Wi-Fi, find dead spots and the best router spot. No ads.

**Full description** (4000 max):

WifiLens shows you how your Wi-Fi really reaches every room, and what to do about it.

ANALYZE THE AIR AROUND YOU
• Every nearby network with signal, band, channel and security
• Spectrum view of crowded channels, plus the busiest hours of the day
• 24-hour signal history for any network
• One-tap health check: weak signal, crowded channel, a better band, a stronger access point, mesh, and security problems such as open or WEP networks, each with a plain-language fix

MAP YOUR HOME
• Draw your floor plan on a simple grid: rooms, walls of different materials, doors
• Place your router and your devices, and see the plan in 3D
• Walk survey: tap where you stand to record the real signal, and calibrate the predictions to your home

DIAGNOSE AND FIX
• Predicted coverage for every room and a list of problem spots
• Best spot: find where to move the router so your weakest device gets the most signal
• Speed test, with history by hour of day
• Live signal meter to walk around with
• Share a coverage report as a PDF or image

AT A GLANCE
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile with your current signal

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Scans, plans and history stay on your phone. The internet is used only for the optional speed test.

Android asks for location permission because it only shows nearby Wi-Fi networks to apps that have it. WifiLens never reads or stores your location.
