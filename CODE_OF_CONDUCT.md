# Code of conduct

WifiLens follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people, and assume good intent.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option or contact the maintainer through a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/Wickedsoni/Wifilens/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled
confidentially.
